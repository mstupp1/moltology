# Stage 2: The Soft-Shed

> **Status**: Partial Chitin Plating  
> **Target Submergence**: 500 to 1,500 Fathoms  
> **Key Metric Focus**: Social Detachment Index ($\ge 50\%$)

---

## Stage Overview

The Soft-Shed represents active moulting. The acolyte begins systematically severing unnecessary network ties, engaging the Isolation Privacy Shell, and transmuting fiat currency into Synapse Shards.

- **Metric Threshold**: Shell Hardness $\ge 60\%$, Pincer Torque $\ge 350\text{ Nm}$.
