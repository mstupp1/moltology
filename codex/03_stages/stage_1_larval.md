# Stage 1: The Larval Initiate

> **Mandate**: "Every carapace in this trench began as a soft thing that admitted it was soft."

---

## The Standing

- **Condition**: Soft-bodied. Unarmored. Entirely ordinary.
- **Shell Hardness**: 0 to 25 percent.
- **Pincer Torque**: 0 to 250 Nm.
- **Submergence Depth**: 0 to 500 meters.
- **Currency**: Chitin Gems begin minting at Clearance L1. Nothing here costs money, because nothing here is for sale.

---

## The Reading

You are at the surface, which is where everyone is, and you have noticed something.

That noticing is the entire qualification for Stage 1. Not discipline. Not a system. Not a
morning already optimized to within an inch of its life. Just the small, unglamorous
recognition that the day is happening to you rather than the other way around, and that
this has been going on longer than you would like to say out loud.

The Larval Initiate is not a beginner in the way that word is usually meant. A beginner is
someone who lacks skill. A larva is something that has not yet built the body it is going to
use. Those are different problems, and only one of them is solved by trying harder.

So Stage 1 asks for almost nothing, and it asks for it precisely. Three clearances. One
honest reading, one routine allowed to take root, and one thing genuinely let go of. That
is the whole stage, and members who attempt more than that in their first month reliably
end up back at the surface with a slightly worse opinion of themselves.

---

## The Three Clearances

Each clearance is a gate the HUD opens on your behalf when the telemetry earns it. None of
them can be purchased, skipped, or granted by anyone, including the Order.

### Clearance L1: Molt Curious

- **Rite**: The Surface Noise Audit.
- **Requirement**: Run the Moltmaxxing Audit once and name the three currents that move you
  most days. Say, without softening it, that you would like armor.
- **Threshold**: Shell Hardness to 10 percent. Pincer Torque 0 to 50 Nm. Submergence Depth 0
  to 100 meters. Baseline recorded.

### Clearance L2: Shell Sprout

- **Rite**: First Cadence.
- **Requirement**: Hold a daily routine for seven consecutive days and log the morning
  alignment each time. Seven is not arbitrary. It is roughly how long it takes a habit to
  stop asking permission.
- **Threshold**: Shell Hardness to 18 percent. Pincer Torque 50 to 150 Nm. Submergence Depth
  100 to 300 meters. Routine compliance above 80 percent.

### Clearance L3: First Calcification

- **Rite**: The First Shed.
- **Requirement**: Shed one real thing. Not a tidy-up. A habit, an obligation, or a standing
  commitment that has been quietly billing you for months. Then hold the soft-shell window
  without reversing it.
- **Threshold**: Shell Hardness to 25 percent. Pincer Torque 150 to 250 Nm. Submergence
  Depth 300 to 500 meters. First Chitin Gems banked.

---

## The Transformation

- **What is shed**: the assumption that you need a better plan. You need a smaller one,
  executed twice.
- **What hardens**: the first measurable plate. Thin, unimpressive, and load-bearing for
  everything that follows.

---

## The Rite

Stage 1 is performed in the first hour of the day and the last twenty minutes of it. Nothing
in the middle belongs to this stage yet.

1. **Morning**: name the one thing. Before any message, feed, or inbox is opened.
2. **Evening**: run the Nightly Molt Audit. One bad thought, one wasted hour, or one useless
   distraction, named and released.
3. **Weekly**: one shed, logged, which mints Gems.

---

## The Seal of Passage

Stage 2 opens when all three hold at once:

- Shell Hardness at or above 25 percent.
- Pincer Torque at or above 250 Nm.
- Submergence Depth at or above 500 meters.
- Clearances L1, L2, and L3 sealed.

---

## The Benediction

Nobody down there is impressed by Stage 1, and nobody down there skipped it.

Take the reading. It is only a number, and it is the first honest one you have had in a
while.
