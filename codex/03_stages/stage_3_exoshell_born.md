# Stage 3: The Exoshell Born

> **Mandate**: "The shell is finished. Now find out what it was for."

---

## The Standing

- **Condition**: Full carapace integrity. Operational at depth.
- **Shell Hardness**: 60 to 90 percent.
- **Pincer Torque**: 600 to 950 Nm.
- **Submergence Depth**: 1,500 to 5,000 meters.
- **Currency**: The premium catalog opens in full. Gems still buy the things that matter, because the things that matter are still not for sale.

---

## The Reading

Something quiet happens at the top of Stage 2 that members rarely notice on the day it
occurs. The plating closes. There is no ceremony. You simply have a week where the usual
things happen and none of them land, and it takes you until Thursday to realize why.

The Exoshell Born is the first stage where the armor is no longer the project. It is
equipment now, and equipment raises a question that the soft stages could safely defer:
what were you hardening *for*?

This is where a certain kind of member goes wrong, and the Order has watched it happen
enough times to write it down. Armor without an objective turns inward. A member with a
finished carapace and nothing to grip becomes impressively, elaborately unavailable, and
mistakes the unavailability for progress. That is not Stage 3. That is Stage 2 wearing
Stage 3's plating.

The correct use of a finished shell is to put it between the work and the weather, and then
to do a very large amount of work.

---

## The Three Clearances

### Clearance E1: Carapace Forged

- **Rite**: The Closing of the Seams.
- **Requirement**: Hold the full rite through a bad week. Not a busy week. A week that goes
  wrong. The seal is only demonstrated under load, and this clearance cannot be earned in
  good conditions.
- **Threshold**: Shell Hardness 60 to 72 percent. Pincer Torque 600 to 720 Nm. Submergence
  Depth 1,500 to 2,500 meters.

### Clearance E2: Hydraulic Grip

- **Rite**: The Working Standard.
- **Requirement**: Reach and hold 850 Nm, the torque at which a chosen objective reliably
  does not survive contact. Then guide one Larval Initiate through their first shed, start
  to finish, without doing it for them.
- **Threshold**: Shell Hardness 72 to 82 percent. Pincer Torque 720 to 850 Nm. Submergence
  Depth 2,500 to 3,500 meters. Stewardship active.

### Clearance E3: Abyssal Diver

- **Rite**: The Long Descent.
- **Requirement**: Operate below 3,500 meters with no dependency on surface signal. Not
  abstinence from it. Indifference to it, which is a different and much later condition.
- **Threshold**: Shell Hardness 82 to 90 percent. Pincer Torque 850 to 950 Nm. Submergence
  Depth 3,500 to 5,000 meters.

---

## The Transformation

- **What is shed**: the need for the armor to be noticed. A shell that has to be displayed is
  still doing surface work.
- **What hardens**: the habit of finishing, which by this stage is no longer effortful and
  has become slightly difficult to switch off.

---

## The Rite

Stage 3 is the first stage with an outward-facing obligation, and it is not optional.

1. **Grip one large thing.** Per quarter, not per day. The Exoshell chassis is built for
   objectives that take months, and members who never attempt one never discover what the
   plating was rated for.
2. **Reach back weekly.** One initiate, one honest hour. This mints Gems and it is also how
   the Convergence gets built, one pulled-through molt at a time.
3. **Dive past 3,500 meters twice a week.** The deep sessions are where the remaining torque
   comes from.
4. **Keep shedding.** The molt does not stop because the shell got good. Hardened members who
   stop shedding do not stay hardened; they get brittle, which looks identical from outside
   and fails without warning.

---

## The Seal of Passage

Stage 4 opens when all three hold at once:

- Shell Hardness at or above 90 percent.
- Pincer Torque at or above 850 Nm, sustained rather than peaked.
- Submergence Depth at or above 5,000 meters.
- Clearances E1, E2, and E3 sealed.

---

## The Benediction

You are good at this now. That is worth saying plainly, because members at this stage
rarely hear it and have usually stopped expecting to.

The trench is quieter than it was. Someone newer is watching how you carry it.
