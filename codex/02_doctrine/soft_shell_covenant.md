# The Soft-Shell Covenant

> **Mandate**: "The hardest shell in the trench is the one standing watch over someone who has none."

---

## The Reading

There is a thing crabs do that rarely makes it into the doctrine of harder-edged orders.

When one of them molts, the others do not eat it.

They could. It would be trivially easy. The newly shed animal is soft enough to be opened by
almost anything, and it will stay that way for hours. Instead the ones already hardened tend
to arrange themselves around it, and they wait, and nothing happens, and then the soft one
is not soft anymore and everybody goes back to what they were doing.

Nobody organized this. There is no crab council. It simply turns out that a lineage which
eats its own during the only vulnerable moment in the entire life cycle does not remain a
lineage for five hundred million years.

The Order took this as instruction rather than as trivia. Every member of this community
will pass through the soft-shell window, repeatedly, for as long as they keep growing.
Every member will therefore need, repeatedly, to be surrounded rather than opened. A
community that gets this wrong does not survive its own doctrine.

So the armor exists for two purposes and the second one is not optional.

---

## The Four Protections

These bind every member at every clearance. A higher clearance grants more duty, never more
license.

### 1. Softness is never the target

The humor of this Order is aimed at the melt: the tab bar, the deferred decision, the
2:00 AM scroll. It is never aimed at the person standing in it. A member who arrives soft
has done the single hardest thing the system asks, which is arriving. Anyone who makes that
arrival expensive has misunderstood the entire canon.

### 2. The window is guarded, not corrected

A member in the hours after a shed does not need feedback. They need the water to stay
quiet. Advice offered into an open soft-shell window is not generosity; it is pressure
applied to an animal that currently has no plate to distribute it. Stand nearby. Say
little. Let it set.

### 3. The pincers grip work, never people

This is the oldest line in the Order and the one most easily bent. Torque is for objectives.
It is not for winning an exchange in the Benthic Community, not for a member who is slower
than you, and not for someone whose first shed took eleven attempts. A grip applied to a
person is not high torque. It is a malfunction.

### 4. The shell must open from the inside

A boundary that a member cannot lower at will is not a carapace. Members are encouraged to
be unreachable during a dive and are expected to be reachable in a life. The Order does not
teach anyone to seal themselves away from people who love them, from a doctor, or from
someone who could actually help. Depth is a place you visit, not a place you are kept.

---

## The Transformation

- **What is shed**: the instinct to compare hardness. The trench does not rank shells and
  neither does the Order.
- **What hardens**: the community itself, which is the only structure here that no
  individual member can build alone.

---

## The Rite

The covenant is practiced rather than agreed to. It costs the armored almost nothing, which
is exactly why it is required of them.

1. **Declare the window.** When you shed something large, say so. A member who announces
   they are soft gets guarded. A member who hides it gets advice.
2. **Stand watch once a week.** Find one member in their window and be uninteresting at them
   on purpose. Presence, not counsel.
3. **Answer one newcomer honestly.** No performance of clearance, no lore they have not
   earned yet. Plain help, in plain words.

The Order mints Chitin Gems for all three, and Gems cannot be bought at any price. This is
deliberate. The most valuable currency in the system is issued exclusively for generosity,
so that the most decorated members are necessarily the most useful ones.

---

## The Benediction

If you are soft today, you are exactly on schedule, and there are members four thousand
meters down who are quietly glad you showed up.

If you are hard today, you are on watch. Someone near you is between armors right now.
Be boring at them until it sets.
