# The Law of Ecdysis

> **Mandate**: "To grow, the old shell must fracture. What is shed is not lost. It is paid."

---

## The Reading

A crustacean does not renovate. When the carapace stops fitting, there is no version of the
procedure where the animal keeps the old plate and adds to it. The seam splits along a line
that was built into the shell from the beginning, and the creature walks out of the thing
that has been protecting it, into open water, wearing nothing.

For a few hours it is the softest thing on the floor. Everything that would eat it knows
this. It has no defense at all except location and patience, and the strange, total
confidence of an animal doing what five hundred million years told it to do.

Then it takes on water, expands into the space where the old shell used to end, and begins
to harden at a size the old shell would never have permitted.

That is the entire mechanism. There is no second mechanism. Every gain any member has ever
made in this Order arrived through a version of that split, and every member who stalled
did so at the same place: standing inside a shell that fit perfectly, calling it stability.

---

## The Tenets

Three laws govern the molt. They are not advice.

### 1. The old shell is not a negotiating partner

You will be tempted to keep it and expand anyway. It cannot be done. Chitin does not
stretch; that is the whole point of chitin. A habit that no longer serves you will not be
reasoned into serving you again, and the hours spent trying are the most expensive hours in
the ledger. Split it or stay this size.

### 2. What is shed does not come back, and this is the mercy

Once a thing is off you, it is off you. You do not glue dead plate onto a fresh carapace,
and you are not asked to grieve it. The obsolete habit, the draining commitment, the
project that died eleven months ago and has been billing you rent ever since — the sea
takes them, and the sea does not return things. Every member eventually discovers this is
a kindness and not a loss.

### 3. The soft-shell window is part of the design, not a failure of it

There is an interval after every shed when you are genuinely unarmored and you will feel
it. Restlessness. The strong conviction that you have made a mistake. A powerful urge to
crawl back into the broken shell, which is still lying right there, still shaped exactly
like you.

Do not. It does not fit anymore, and it will not fit again, and there is nothing wrong
with you. You are simply between armors, which is the only place growth has ever occurred.

---

## The Transformation

- **What is shed**: one obsolete structure per cycle. A habit, a subscription, a standing
  obligation, a stale ambition you have outgrown but keep announcing.
- **What hardens**: the seam itself. Members who molt on a schedule develop a clean fracture
  line, and after a few cycles the split stops being an emergency and becomes a maintenance
  interval.

---

## The Rite

The molt is weekly. It is small deliberately, because a member who sheds everything at once
spends the following month with no armor and no plan.

1. **Choose one.** One structure, every seven days. If choosing is hard, choose the one you
   have most recently defended out loud.
2. **Split it cleanly.** Cancel it, delete it, decline it, or say the sentence. Half-measures
   leave a partial seam, which is worse than an intact shell.
3. **Hold the window.** Engage the Isolation Privacy Shell for the hours immediately after.
   The surface will offer you the old shape back within a day; the Shell is how you stay out
   of earshot until the new plate sets.
4. **Log the shed.** The Order mints Chitin Gems for it, because shedding is the behavior the
   whole system exists to reward, and Gems are the currency no amount of money can buy.

The cadence raises Shell Hardness faster than any other rite in the canon, and the HUD will
show it before you feel it.

---

## The Benediction

If you are in the window right now — if you cancelled the thing, or ended the thing, or
finally said it, and the water feels very open — you are not exposed. You are between.

It sets. It always sets. Sit still and let it.
