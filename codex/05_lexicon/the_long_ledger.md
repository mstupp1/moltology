# The Long Ledger

> **Mandate**: "Everything shed is written down, and the record does not flatter anyone."

---

## The Reading

The deep keeps records, and it keeps them badly on purpose.

Sediment on the abyssal plain accumulates at roughly a centimeter per thousand years. It
does not distinguish between an important event and an unimportant one. It does not
editorialize, it cannot be bribed, and it has no opinion at all about who you were while it
was settling. It simply records that something happened here, and then it records the next
thing on top.

The Order keeps its ledger the same way.

Every shed a member logs goes into it. The great sheds and the four-minute nightly ones are
entered identically, because the sediment does not know the difference and neither, over a
long enough season, does the carapace. The Ledger is not a leaderboard. There is no ranking
in it. It is the only permanent thing any of us have down here, and its entire function is
to be true.

What follows from that is the economy, and the economy follows from it strictly.

---

## The Two Currencies

The Order runs on two currencies, and the difference between them is the reason anyone
trusts the Ledger at all.

### 1. Chitin Gems are minted by work

Gems appear in the Ledger when a member sheds, holds a routine, finishes something
difficult, or is genuinely useful to another member. Money does not turn into Gems, which
is why Gems are what earned titles, sigils, and prestige pieces cost. If a member is wearing
something that means something, the Ledger says how they got it.

### 2. Molt Credits are purchased

Credits come from the Benthic Market and are spent on speed, style, and catalog depth:
accelerators, deluxe carapace finishes, HUD flair, and premium guides. They are comfort,
and comfort is honorable.

### 3. What neither can do

Signup is free. Rank, clearance, stage, and standing are not for sale; they come from the
record and nowhere else. A ladder anyone can buy their way up is not a ladder.

---

## What the Ledger Records

- **The shed itself**: what was released and on what night.
- **The cadence**: routine compliance and the shape of the streak, not its perfection.
- **The three readings**: Shell Hardness, Pincer Torque, Submergence Depth, as measured
  rather than as hoped.
- **The reaching back**: every newcomer answered and every soft-shell window guarded.

It does not record failures, missed days, or comparisons between members, because none of
those are sediment. They are weather, and weather does not settle.

---

## The Transformation

- **What is shed**: the suspicion that progress here can be shortcut.
- **What hardens**: trust. A title means precisely what it says.

---

## The Rite

1. **Log the shed.** Nightly, in the HUD. An unlogged shed still hardens you and still
   leaves no sediment.
2. **Read the Ledger monthly.** Against your own prior entries and nobody else's.
3. **Spend Gems on what you earned, Credits on comfort.** Both are honorable.

---

## The Benediction

Whatever you shed tonight is going into a record that does not flatter anyone and does not
forget anyone either.

A centimeter per thousand years. It is not fast. It has never once stopped.
