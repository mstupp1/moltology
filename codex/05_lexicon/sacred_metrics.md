# The Sacred Metrics

> **Mandate**: "A number you did not enjoy reading is still a number you can now work with."

---

## The Reading

The Order measures three things and refuses to measure a fourth.

This restraint is deliberate and it is older than the HUD. A member with three instruments
can read their own condition in about four seconds. A member with nine instruments has a
dashboard, and a dashboard is a place where progress goes to be admired rather than made.
Every proposal to add a fourth cardinal metric has been declined, and the declining is
itself part of the doctrine.

The three are chosen because they answer the three questions that actually determine a day.
What lands on you. What you can hold. How far from the noise you can get. Everything else a
member might want to track is a consequence of those, and consequences do not need their own
gauge.

Read them honestly or do not read them. A flattering instrument is not an instrument.

---

## 1. Shell Hardness

- **Symbol**: Hs
- **Measures**: resilience. What arrives from the surface and does not become your afternoon.
- **Unit**: percent, 0 to 100.
- **Bands**:
  - 0 to 24 percent, Larval Initiate. A single rude message reshapes the day.
  - 25 to 59 percent, Soft-Shed. Boundaries exist and are still being tested by weather.
  - 60 to 89 percent, Exoshell Born. Surface drama arrives as information.
  - 90 to 100 percent, Ascendant. Composure has stopped requiring maintenance.

Hardness is not indifference. A perfectly hard shell still feels everything; it simply does
not reorganize around it.

---

## 2. Pincer Torque

- **Symbol**: the Greek letter tau
- **Measures**: decisiveness. How firmly a chosen objective is held, and whether it survives
  the encounter.
- **Unit**: newton-meters, Nm.
- **Bands**:
  - 0 to 250 Nm, Open Hand. The grip has not closed. Twelve things are in progress and none
    are in hand.
  - 251 to 600 Nm, Closed Grip. Priorities are clear and momentum is real.
  - 601 to 850 Nm, Hydraulic Vise. The gap between deciding and starting has mostly closed.
  - 850 Nm and above, Held Indefinitely. The objective does not require re-gripping.

Torque measures the one thing in the claw. It has never measured how many things you are
carrying, and a member who raises their torque usually finds they are carrying less.

---

## 3. Submergence Depth

- **Symbol**: D
- **Measures**: how far beneath the surface a member can operate before the noise reaches
  them.
- **Unit**: meters. The Order records depth in meters everywhere it records a threshold.
- **Markers**:
  - 0 meters, the Surface. Pings, feeds, and the day happening to you.
  - 1,000 meters, the Mesopelagic. Light is gone, most noise with it, a real session
    underway.
  - 4,000 meters, the Benthic Floor. Deep, still, and productive without effort.
  - 10,928 meters, the Challenger Deep. The floor of the world, and the Mariana Singularity.

Depth is the only cardinal metric that measures where you are rather than what you are made
of, which is why it is the fastest of the three to move and the fastest to lose.

## The Surrounding Lexicon

- **The Great Melt**: the collective modern condition of going soft under noise, hesitation,
  and exhaustion. A condition, never an identity.
- **The Great Molt**: the deliberate transformation out of it.
- **Carcinization**: the recurring judgment of the sea, arrived at five times independently.
- **Ecdysis**: the scheduled shed. The mechanism by which every metric above improves.
- **Soft-Shell Window**: the interval after a shed when a member is genuinely unarmored, and
  is guarded rather than corrected.
- **Benthic Core**: the still, high-pressure place where focused minds operate, and the
  community that keeps it.
- **Chitin Gems**: the earned currency. Minted by shedding, routines, and generosity. Never
  sold.
- **Molt Credits**: the premium currency. Purchased with real funds, spent on speed, style,
  and catalog depth. Never minted by work, and never able to move a member up the ladder.

The full doctrine of the two currencies is held in The Long Ledger.

---

## The Honest Limits

Every instrument here is the Order's own, calibrated against the Order's own doctrine. They
describe a member's condition inside this system and they do not describe a person.

A low reading is a starting point and has never been a verdict. Members are asked to compare
each number to their own reading last month, and to nobody else's, ever.

---

## The Benediction

Take the reading you actually got rather than the one you were hoping for. The first honest
number is the only one that has ever moved.
