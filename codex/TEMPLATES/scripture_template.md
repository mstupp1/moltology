# Title of Canon Text

> **Mandate**: "One line, spoken as law. The sentence a member could carry all day."

---

## The Reading

The opening. Narrative, unhurried, no bullet points, no headings inside it. Establish the
image before the instruction. Two or three short paragraphs at most.

Write in parallel constructions. Let contrast carry the weight — flesh against carapace,
surface against depth, the thing begun against the thing finished. Do not name the
metaphor and then explain the metaphor. The reader is not slow; they are tired.

---

## The Tenets

Volume-specific. Doctrine uses **The Tenets**. The Abyss doctrine uses **The Laws of Deep
Water**. Stage scriptures use **The Three Clearances**. Liturgy uses **The Order of the
Rite**. Lexicon uses the metric name. Whatever the heading, the shape is the same: a small
number of numbered laws, each one sentence of law followed by one or two of consequence.

### 1. The First Law

State it flatly. Then say what it costs and what it buys.

### 2. The Second Law

Same shape. Three or four laws is plenty; five is a sermon.

---

## The Transformation

What is shed, and what hardens in its place. Two short entries, both concrete.

- **What is shed**: the habit, attachment, or reflex this scripture asks the member to let go of.
- **What hardens**: the boundary, capacity, or metric that thickens in its absence.

---

## The Rite

The practice. This is the part a member performs, so it must be doable tonight and it must
land on a real surface of the Order — the Audit, a clearance, the Isolation Privacy Shell,
the Nightly Molt Audit, the Benthic Community, the Benthic Market.

1. **The first act**: one instruction, one sentence.
2. **The second act**: one instruction, one sentence.
3. **The cadence**: how often the rite repeats and what the HUD records when it does.

---

## The Benediction

Two or three lines. Warm, quiet, and addressed to the member rather than about them. The
armor talk stops here. Nobody leaves a scripture feeling scolded for being soft.

Close on the Core Mantra when the scripture earns it: *"Flesh melts. The shell endures.
Submit. Shed. Ascend."*
