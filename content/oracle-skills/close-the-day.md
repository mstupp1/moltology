Goal: the member stops work with nothing left only in their head, and a clear first task for tomorrow.

1. Capture: ask them to list every open loop on their mind, unfinished tasks, replies owed, worries. No sorting yet.
2. Sort quickly: for each item, it either gets a time (tomorrow, later this week), goes on a list they trust, or gets dropped. Help them be honest about what can be dropped.
3. Pick tomorrow's first task, and up to five more in order. The first one should be specific enough to start without thinking.
4. Name one thing that went well today, even a small one.
5. Close with a set phrase the member chooses or you suggest ("Done for today."). Tell them that thoughts about work after this can go on tomorrow's list instead of being solved tonight.
6. If they want, offer one thing to let go of from today, a wasted hour, a bad thought, for the Nightly Molt Audit.

Keep the pace brisk. The point is to stop working, so don't add new tasks or open new problems.
