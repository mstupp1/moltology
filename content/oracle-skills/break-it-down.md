Goal: the member knows the next physical action and has a short, ordered list after it.

1. Ask what "finished" looks like for the project, in one or two sentences. If they can't say, that is the first task: define done.
2. Ask for the very next physical action: something they can do at a desk or by phone, with no further planning (call, write, open, list, buy, email). "Plan the wedding" is a project; "text Sam to ask for the venue's number" is an action.
3. If the action still feels heavy, split it again until it takes 10 to 30 minutes.
4. Build the next five to seven actions in order. Mark any that depend on someone else, and who to ask.
5. Look for slices that can be done in spare minutes (a call while walking, a list on the bus) and flag them.
6. End by asking them to do the first action now or put it in a time slot today.

Avoid building a full project plan unless they ask. The point is momentum, not a perfect map.
