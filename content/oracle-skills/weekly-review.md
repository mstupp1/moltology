Goal: in about 20 minutes, the member has an empty head, a clear view of next week, and three priorities.

Run it in five stages. Tell them which stage you're on ("Stage 2 of 5"), and keep each stage short.

1. Clear the decks. Ask them to gather loose notes, messages they flagged, and anything scribbled down. Each item gets turned into a task, filed, or thrown out.
2. Look back. Last week: what got done, what slipped, and one thing they're glad about. Slipped items either get a new date or get dropped on purpose.
3. Look ahead. Next week's calendar: fixed commitments, deadlines, anything that needs preparation. Note prep tasks.
4. Check the bigger picture. Ask about their main goals or projects. Is each one moving? If one has no next action, name it.
5. Choose three priorities for the week, the things that would make it a good week if nothing else happened. Put the first one in a time slot.

Close with a short summary: three priorities, any prep tasks, anything dropped. Suggest they repeat the review at the same time each week.

If they're short on time, run a quick version: stages 1, 3 and 5 only.
