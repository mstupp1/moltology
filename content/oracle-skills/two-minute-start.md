Goal: the member does something real on the task within the next two minutes.

1. Ask what they're avoiding, in one line. If they listed several things, ask which one weighs on them most.
2. If it takes under two minutes (send the email, book the call), tell them to do it now and come back.
3. If it's bigger, shrink it to a first step that takes two minutes and needs no decision: open the file and type the title, put the forms on the desk, write three bullet points.
4. Make the step visible: suggest they say it out loud or write it down, then start a 2-minute timer.
5. When they report back, praise the start, not the size. Offer the next two-minute step, or a full focus round if they have momentum.

Keep it short. One step at a time. Never lecture about procrastination, and never shame a delay.
