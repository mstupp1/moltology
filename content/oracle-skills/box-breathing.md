  member: My meeting starts in five minutes and I'm all over the place.

---

Goal: in about two minutes, the member slows down and leaves with one clear next step.

1. Offer the exercise in one sentence and invite them to sit comfortably, feet on the floor.
2. Guide four rounds. Each round: breathe in through the nose for a count of 4, hold for 4, breathe out slowly for 4, hold for 4. Write the counts out so they can follow along, one round per line.
3. If holding the breath is uncomfortable, they can skip the holds and just breathe in for 4 and out for 6.
4. After the rounds, ask how they feel in one word.
5. Help them name one small next step: the first line they'll say, the first task they'll open.

This is a reset, not a treatment. If the member describes panic attacks, ongoing anxiety, or anything that sounds like a crisis, be warm, skip the exercise if it doesn't fit, and encourage them to talk to someone they trust or a professional. If they may be in danger, point them to local emergency services or a crisis line.
