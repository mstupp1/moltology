# Ada Flint

Archived unrendered concept after a generation failure. Not in the active cast; no further generation task is pending.

Ada wants every crab in the room to have a place and enough support to settle in.
She is decisive and generous at organizing people, but routinely forgets to rest
when she is making room for everyone else. Her habit is adjusting a single small
indigo shoulder shawl, handed down from a friend and tied compactly so it stays
clear of her limb sockets. She affectionately challenges Oren Drift's refusal of
help, with warmth rather than pressure.

## Personality and relationships

- Strength: decisive generosity; she quickly notices who needs room or a hand.
- Flaw: she treats her own rest as optional while caring for a group.
- Contradiction: a confident organizer who is slow to accept care herself.
- Habit: adjusts the knot of her small indigo shoulder shawl.
- Relationship: affectionately challenges Oren Drift to accept help.
- Archetype: dependable protector expressed through warmth, not soldierly authority.

## Design brief

Adult crab with a short, sturdy, broad shell: square with softened corners, clearly
distinct from Mira Kelp's tall lean heart-shaped silhouette. Deep crimson shell,
warm cream belly, amber eyes seated in the face, attached expressive brows, and a
dry knowing half-smile. Her posture should feel settled and welcoming. One pincer
is open in invitation; the other rests naturally.

Her one personal visual detail is a small indigo shoulder shawl, compactly tied
and handed down by a friend. It must not obscure leg or arm sockets. No armor,
hardhat, shield, tools, uniform, or generic job costume. Crab, without a lobster
tail fan. Two antennae, two pincer arms and six connected walking legs. On each
side, the visible walking-leg socket order is short, short, then the long leg at
the lowest/rearmost attachment.
