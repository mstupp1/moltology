# Bram Silt

Retired character concept (2026-11-04, at the user’s request). Do not generate or
include in the active cast. No render or canonical registration was created.
The brief and failed attempts remain here as a historical record.

Bram packs for every possible mishap. By the end of a journey, he has usually given
most of it away. He checks the clasp on his satchel twice before leaving, then
asks whether anyone else has forgotten anything.

He wants everyone to get home safely. He notices what others need and plans
carefully, but he struggles to leave while anything remains uncertain. His story
moves forward when he trusts a companion enough to take the next step before the
whole route is settled.

On his first journey, another traveler turned back to bring him a spare lantern.
He has been repaying that small kindness to other travelers ever since. The spare
he packed for himself is usually the first thing he gives away.

## Personality and relationships

- **Strength:** patient, observant, reliable; remembers the small things others forget.
- **Flaw:** prepares past the point of usefulness and mistakes another checklist for progress.
- **Contradiction:** fiercely organized about supplies, immediately generous with them.
- **Habit:** checks the satchel clasp twice, even when he knows it is shut.
- **Proposed cast relationship:** quietly packs spares for the bashful lobster
  (`lobster_peek`), whose willingness to try before feeling ready challenges his caution.
  This is a draft story relationship, not an approved change to that mascot's identity.
- **Archetype:** reluctant adventurer and dependable companion. Original design and
  behavior; no borrowed fictional costume, likeness or catchphrase.

recognizable pincers. No occupational uniform, armor, tool belt or extra props.
