# Gus Pebble

**State:** archived unrendered concept after one generation failure. Not in the active cast.

Gus is the friend who remembers how everyone likes their tea kelp and which
stories make them laugh. He wants the whole crew to feel welcome, but says yes
to too many gatherings and has to learn to leave a little quiet in his week.
When choosing a story, he gently taps his pincers together while he thinks.

## Personality and relationships

- **Strength:** quietly hospitable and attentive to other people's preferences.
- **Flaw:** overcommits to gatherings because he hates disappointing anyone.
- **Contradiction:** makes everyone feel at home, then forgets to leave time for himself.
- **Habit:** taps his pincers together while choosing a story.
- **Relationship:** a steady, welcoming friend to Mira Kelp and Pip Current; he makes room for their ideas and invites them to slow down over a shared story.
- **Archetype:** the generous host learning to set a kind boundary.

## Design brief

Adult crab with a broad low rounded shell, sturdy barrel build and short adult
height. Warm tangerine satin shell, pale cream belly and amethyst eyes. Calm,
amused eyelids and an easy grin. One small cluster of pale freckles above one
brow, a subtle family resemblance detail. No gear, clothing or costume.

Crab anatomy: two pincer arms and six walking legs, three per side. On each side,
read the sockets top/front to bottom/rear as short, short, then the final long
supporting leg at the lowest/rearmost socket. Two antennae; no lobster tail fan.
The broad low rounded silhouette should remain distinct from Mira Kelp's tall,
heart-shaped jade shell.
