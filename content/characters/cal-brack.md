# Cal Brack

Generated individual selected for the requested six-character replacement cast.

Cal wants a disagreement to end with everyone still willing to sit together.
He is good at finding the practical point two companions share, but avoids
admitting when he is frustrated. He can make room for every opinion except his
own. Before finally speaking up, he pauses and lifts one pincer a little.

His small uneven cream forehead patch resembles his father's. As a young crab
he tried to hide it beneath his brows; now he likes seeing a familiar face in
his own reflection. It is natural shell coloration, not an emblem or decal.

Cal gives Mira's plans a patient hearing and helps Pip finish a thought without
being rushed. His quiet confidence becomes more useful when he lets them know
what he actually wants.

## Design brief

Adult crab, regular height and classic sturdy build, broad softly square shell,
cobalt-blue satin chitin, pale ice-blue underside, topaz-gold eyes seated in the
face, attached expressive brows and dry gentle half-smile. Two curved antennae,
two classic pincer arms, two flank spikes per side, no lobster tail fan. Six
walking legs: each side has two short legs followed by the final long supporting
leg at the lowest/rearmost socket. All tips readable, sockets unobscured.

One irregular cream forehead patch; no costume or belongings. One pincer lifted
in a quiet invitation to speak. Distinct square blue silhouette versus lean
heart-shaped green Mira and slender lavender Pip.
