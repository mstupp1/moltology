### 17 Shop Collections and Discounted Bundles
- Added 85 new 3D gear pieces across 17 live themed collections, introducing coats, hoodies, blazers, sweaters, scarves, capes, footwear, eyewear, hats, and held props in varied weaves and prints.
- Added a collection filter in the Shop, a featured Collection of the Week spotlight, and one-click collection bundles at a 20% discount that only charge for pieces you do not already own.
- Connected matching gear collections directly beneath each benefit guide so visitors can try on or wear the look.

### Expanded 3D Character Customization
- Added 13 new shell colors, an optional tip fade, and four new shell finishes: Matte, Velvet, Beetle sheen, and Crystal.
- Introduced new patterned shell markings including Coral lace, Tide rings, Reef camo, Scales, Rosettes, Flames, Honeycomb, Barnacles, Ripples, Constellation, and Marble.
- Expanded face customization with eye colors, mismatched eyes, new eye shapes, adjustable eyebrows, cheek blush, and an open-smile cheer.
