### Protect Your Account
- Automatic linking through Google sign-in now requires a verified email on the existing account.
- New passwords must contain at least eight characters.

### Keep Conversations Useful
- Article comments now pass bot checks, posting limits, and moderation before being accepted.
- Comments can only be added to published articles.

### Choose Your Subscriptions
- Newsletter requests made through the contact form now send an email confirmation link.
- The Oracle gives a clear failure message when its usage allowance cannot be checked.
