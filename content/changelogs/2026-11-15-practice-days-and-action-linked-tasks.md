### Practice Days and Action-Linked Tasks
- Set your weekly practice days in Settings so scheduled rest days protect your streak instead of breaking it.
- Daily tasks now connect directly to real actions across the app, including focus and breathing timers, saving your top priority, helping in the forum, and finishing Academy lessons.
- Completed tasks stay checked off cleanly, with each practice name shown beneath its title.

### Livelier 3D Characters
- Added springy movement to antennae, claws, tails, and hats, smoother bending joints, and natural turning steps when rotating your character.
- Character reactions now animate the mouth and brows alongside sharper, higher-detail portraits.

### Smoother Phone Chats
- Fixed the phone keyboard spacing in Oracle chat, moved new chats to a bottom composer, and added a list of recent conversations when starting fresh.
