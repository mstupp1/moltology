### Five Clear Vectors
- Shell, Torque, Synapse, Ecdysis, and Depth are now the only stats on your character.
- Gear bonuses appear beside each number, so you can see exactly what a piece adds.

### A Bottom Bar for Phones
- Dashboard, Oracle, Character, Forum, and More sit at the bottom of the screen.
- More opens a short sheet with Profile, Codex, Academy, support, and your account.

### A Flatter Sidebar
- Daily alignment lives in the sidebar, and the sidebar is one simple list.
- Stages moved into Character, and Friends moved into Profile.
- Your guest audit results and alignment ticks are kept when you sign up.
