### Community Leaderboard and Endless Scroll
- Added a community leaderboard with weekly and all-time rankings, stage filters, and gold, silver, and bronze crests for last week's top three members.
- Forum feeds, boards, and discussion replies now load automatically as you scroll, keeping your place when navigating back.

### An Oracle That Reads Moltology
- The Oracle can now look up public forum threads, news articles, changelog updates, Academy courses, and Codex passages to answer questions with current context.
- Replies display rich interactive cards for everything the Oracle read, alongside rotating starter prompts and a Suggested or More ideas view on new chats.

### Character Proportions and Support
- Spread character height naturally across the body instead of stretching legs alone, with calmer option previews and faster portrait generation.
- Updated the Support page to a two-column desktop layout with recent updates and added an optional tip jar link in the footer, Support page, and account menu.
