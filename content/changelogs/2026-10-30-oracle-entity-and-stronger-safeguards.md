### A New Way to Talk
- Switch between standard chat and a three-dimensional Entity view on the full-page Oracle.
- The Entity responds visually to typing and replies while keeping your conversation context.
- Your preferred view is remembered for your next visit.

### Fairer Limits and Private Drafts
- Simultaneous chat requests now share the same allowance check, keeping usage limits consistent.
- Once-per-email discounts are protected against duplicate claims made at the same time.
- Unpublished articles stay out of public article pages and text downloads.
