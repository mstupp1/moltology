### Open Profiles and Academy Previews
- Visitors without an account can now view member profiles, see equipped gear, and start building their own character from any profile page.
- Every Academy course and lesson page is open to browse, with one free preview lesson per course and a quick sign-up prompt to save progress or take quizzes.

### Unified Visual System
- Updated typography, spacing, and buttons across every page with clean sentence-case labels, rounded filter pills, and consistent top header bands.
- Kept forum topic footers on a single clean row on phones, made daily task checkoffs smoother, and ensured notification alerts always appear above open menus and readers.

### Public Oracle MAX Comparison and Lighter Water
- Redesigned the Oracle MAX page as a side-by-side plan comparison and opened it to everyone as Coming soon.
- Replaced heavy background bubble animations with lighter glass bubbles that pause behind full-screen views and respect reduced-motion preferences.
