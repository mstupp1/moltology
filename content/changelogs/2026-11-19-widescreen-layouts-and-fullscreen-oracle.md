### Widescreen Pages and Aligned Headers
- Widened the main app panel on larger laptops so Shop and Academy grids fill the screen comfortably without awkward margins.
- Aligned header bars, action buttons, and the search bar along one steady row, and extended Dashboard and profile heroes edge to edge on phones.

### Fullscreen Oracle and Touch Scrolling
- Set the Oracle to open fullscreen by default on desktop while still remembering whichever view mode you prefer next.
- Switched touch devices to clean native overlay scrollbars so phone screens keep every pixel of horizontal space.

### Baked 3D Lighting
- Baked soft studio lighting and crease shading directly into 3D characters, the site emblem, and public ocean scenes for richer depth and smoother performance on mobile.
