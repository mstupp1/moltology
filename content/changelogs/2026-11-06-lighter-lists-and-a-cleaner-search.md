### Faster Long Lists
- Forum topics and replies load 30 at a time, with a Load more button when you want to keep reading.
- Forum boards show unread counts faster.

### A Cleaner Search
- The search panel is simpler, and it fills the screen on phones.
- A Go to list option takes you straight to the full page of results.

### Clearer Notifications and Gear
- Notifications have their own bell, so they are easy to find.
- Customize shows only the gear you own, and you can try on new pieces in the shop.
