### Gems From Your Work
- Chitin Gems are now earned from daily alignment, streaks, stage milestones, and first-time achievements.
- Spend your gems on gear you want, with a clear price for each rarity.

### A Rebuilt Market
- Gear is now shown as unlocks, with rarer versions of each piece to collect.
- Filter by slot and rarity, and sort the grid to find what you want quickly.
- A featured banner sits above the grid when nothing is filtered.

### Better Fit and New Store Items
- Hats sit on the head, and wide bodies no longer clash with narrow heads.
- A new hoodie and tote are now in the store.
- On phones, the Creator keeps your character in view while you choose options.
