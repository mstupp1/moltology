### Public Member Profiles & Connections
- View dedicated public profile cards showcasing member avatars, ascension stage, live biometrics, and equipment loadouts.
- Search for fellow crustaceans across the platform and send connection requests from member profiles or the Connections hub.
- Receive persistent connection notifications in the Activity Center with instant accept and decline actions.

- Pin essential transmissions to the top of your chat list for fast access, archive resolved discussions, or delete old sessions.
- Enjoy responsive thread menus and action sheets tailored for both desktop and mobile screens.
