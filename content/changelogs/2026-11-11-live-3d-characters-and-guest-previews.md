### Live 3D Characters Everywhere
- Replaced the flat character drawings with live 3D characters framed in a glass porthole across profiles, the forum, and the top bar.
- Added an interactive progress ring and daily task notches around your portrait so today's progress stays visible at a glance.

### Open Access for Visitors
- Opened the Shop, Academy catalog, support contact form, and a preview of the Oracle to visitors without an account.
- Added one-tap Google sign-in for faster account creation and sign-in.

### Unified Panels and Reader Controls
- Placed app pages on a consistent see-through panel with full-width header bands and a calm ocean background.
- Moved your Profile and Chitin Gems and Molt Credits balances into the account menu, and added selectable text and a dark page toggle to the Codex reader.
