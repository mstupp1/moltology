### One Name for Each Page
- Pages use the same name in the menu, the page heading, and the browser tab.
- Icons are clearer: Character has a shell, Forum uses speech bubbles, and Quiz has a checklist.

### A Floating Top Bar
- The top bar floats above your content on phones and desktop.
- On desktop, the side rail is more compact. On phones, today's alignment count sits inside a small progress ring.

### Activity and Codex
- Activity shows newer items first, grouped under Today, Yesterday, and date headings, and loads more as you scroll.
- The Codex reader is slimmer, with section links, search hits, and a sticky bar on phones.
- Daily alignment tasks now use the names from the Daily Shedding Routine.
