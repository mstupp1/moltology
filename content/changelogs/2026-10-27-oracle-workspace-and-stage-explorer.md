### Keep Your Conversations Close
- Browse and search saved chats beside your conversation on desktop.
- Start a new chat from the header and switch the chat sidebar with clearer controls.
- Select and copy message text again, with a compact button to return to the latest reply.

### Find Your Next Step
- Choose a stage from a horizontal row to see its details without a long stack of expanded sections.
- The selected stage stays in view as you move between stages.

### Chat More Comfortably
- The phone message box has a revised layout, and full-screen conversations use a solid background.
- A simpler model picker makes conversation options easier to scan.
