### Glassy Eyes and Sculpted Finish
- Refined character eyes with a clear glass coat, wider relaxed eyelids, and larger irises, paired with a smooth, studio-lit shell finish.
- Locked shell surface textures firmly to each body part so details stay crisp without shimmering as characters breathe or turn.

### Jumps, Stepping Feet, and Livelier Springs
- Characters now crouch, push off, and land with bent knees when jumping, and lift their feet into realistic steps when changing poses or turning.
- Added livelier spring motion to antennae, claws, tail fan plates, and hats.

### Textured Gear and Fitted Visors
- Added distinct surface materials to 3D gear alongside subtle tone-on-tone engravings on rare and higher-tier pieces.
- Sized visors cleanly to each character's head shape so eyewear always sits neatly in front of the eyes.
