---
title: What to put down this week
summary: A reading on choosing one concrete thing to stop carrying.
kind: reading
duration: 8
---

Most people try to molt by announcing a new identity. The shell does not care about the
announcement. It cares about the weight you stop picking up.

Pick one thing that already feels finished: a tab you reopen out of guilt, a chat you answer out
of fear, a plan you keep rewriting so you never have to start. Write it down in one sentence.
That sentence is the shed.

Leave the rest of the pile alone for seven days. A first shell is small on purpose. If it feels
unimpressive, you are probably doing it correctly.
