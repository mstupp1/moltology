# Archetypes for the 48 benefit topics

Ten costumes, each imitating an industry genre (never one real brand). All ten are built; reuse one before adding an eleventh. The kit key (first column) is the `kit` value in a page file.
"Hook" is the real Moltology thing pages in that kit usually lean on.

| Kit | Status | Industry | Topics | Copy from the genre | Avoid | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| clinic | Built | Telehealth, biohacking, nootropics | Libido, Hardness, Focus, Clarity, Intelligence | Warm paper ground, serif headlines, "online assessment", 3-step how it works, plan picker, comparison table, black pill button | Doctors, prescriptions, pills, invented results, anything explicit | Moltmax quiz as the assessment; daily tasks as the plan; Shell Hardness vector |
| can | Built | Energy drinks, loud snacks | Energy drinks, Energy, Spice, Zest | Saturated grounds, fat caps, stickers, marquee, flavor tiles, nutrition label, hard offset shadows | Health claims, caffeine advice beyond "cut back slowly" | Daily tasks as flavors; Low Tide reset; merch store |
| retreat | Built | Mindfulness apps, wellness retreats | Spirituality, Faith, Freedom, Emotional, Empathy | Script lockup, Garamond, linen and sage, slow pacing, itinerary, one image per screen | Religious claims, telling people what to believe | Codex readings; Sit in silence; Let one thing go |
| atelier | Built | Luxury fashion and beauty editorial | Fashion, Beauty, Sexy, Famous, Stature | Cream or black, big serif, issue numbers, full-bleed portraits, lookbook grid | Body shaming, explicit content | 3D character and the cosmetics shop as "the collection" (lookbook should carry 3+ photos) |
| ledger | Built | Fintech, wealth management | Financial, Investing, Generational, Career, Hustle | Deep green or navy, tabular numbers, charts, "returns" cards, compliance footnotes | Financial advice, real returns, real prices | XP and stage growth as the portfolio; Learn something hard |
| locker | Built | Performance sportswear, supplements | Strength, Physicality, Grip, Fitness, Movement, Resilience | Black and volt, condensed caps, athlete crops, spec tables | Supplement claims | Moltmax vectors as stats; Pincer Torque; The Grip |
| platform | Built | AI SaaS, cybersecurity | Automation, Synergy, AI swarms, Cyber, Adaptive | Dark grid, product UI frames, feature grid, pricing table | Fake customer logos | Oracle chat and skills; real app screenshots (put a real screenshot in the hero frame when one fits) |
| arcade | Built | Gaming and esports | Gaming, Gamification, Technique | Key art, rank badges, patch notes, season pass | Fake player counts | XP ring, stages, cosmetics market |
| seminar | Built | Self-help books, guru seminars | Self help Books, Charisma, Communication, Commitment, Loyalty, Answers, Truth | Book-cover hero, bestseller-style badges (no real lists), pull quotes, "next cohort" | Fake bestseller claims, fake endorsements | Codex as the book; Academy courses |
| network | Built | Social and community apps | Community, Social, Culture | Bright cards, avatar stacks, feed mockups, app-store style badges | Fake member counts | Forum, community group photo, friends (sample posts use cast stills and are labelled as samples) |

Near-twin topics (Energy and Energy drinks, Financial and Investing, Faith and Spirituality,
Social and Community) stay separate pages with different search intents. Check with
`npm run blog:trends` that their target queries differ.
